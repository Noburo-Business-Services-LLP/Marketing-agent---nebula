## OPEN: subtitles are not good enough (owner feedback, 2026-10-18)
The owner watched `fx_ta.mp4` (the old 30 s Tamil video, made BEFORE the repetition fix, with captions added) and said: captions break, are not continuous, do not sync, and the Tamil text is wrong. Findings from a real transcription I ran (OpenAI `whisper-1`, `verbose_json`, segment and word timestamps; about one cent):
- OLD `ta.mp4` (30 s): the model really said different words from our line and repeated one ("வீட்டிருக்கே இலவச மதிப்பு" twice; it said "மதிப்பு" instead of "டெலிவரி") and the speech windows were 2.1 to 8.5 s, 9.7 to 18.5 s, 23.4 to 25.9 s. Our captions showed OUR intended text with ESTIMATED timing (start at 1 s of each clip), so they did not match what was said or when. That is the root cause: captions are not derived from the real audio.
- NEW `one.mp4` (10 s, after the fix): says "இன்றே ஆர்டர் செய்யுங்கள். ஆயிரம் ரூபாய்க்கு மேல் வீட்டிலேயே இலவச டெலிவரி." once, speech 0.0 to 5.5 s (so speech can start at 0 s, not 1 s). Whisper's own Tamil spelling is imperfect ("ஓடர்", "தீபாவலி"), so DISPLAY our intended text, not Whisper's.
- `hi.mp4` (Hindi): said the line correctly once, speech 2.7 to 9.4 s.

**Planned fix (not built; the agent run was skipped because usage hit 90%):** in `services/studioVideoFinish.js` (and the chain), before burning captions, extract the audio of the joined pre-finish video (ffmpeg-static, mono 16 kHz m4a), call `whisper-1` (`response_format=verbose_json`, `timestamp_granularities[]=segment` and `word`, `language` = ta/hi/en/te/kn/ml from the video language; endpoint `https://api.openai.com/v1/audio/transcriptions`, key `OPENAI_API_KEY`, cost about USD 0.006 per minute; put it behind the spend guard and record it like other providers), take for each clip k (clip start offsets come from the join result, 10 s each minus overlaps) the real speech window = first segment start to last segment end inside that clip, then spread the intended line's caption chunks over that REAL window in proportion to chunk length, hold each caption until the next starts, hide after the window ends. If transcription fails or finds no speech: skip subtitles and set a plain notice ("We could not time the subtitles, so this video has none.") rather than show wrong captions. Keep the toggle default OFF until the owner has watched a corrected video. Add tests with a faked transcription; prove it locally on the real files in the scratchpad dir (`ta.mp4`, `one.mp4`, `hi.mp4`, `v20.mp4` from the session scratchpad; if gone, regenerate with the owner's OK). Optionally compare Whisper text with the intended line and skip captions when they are very different.
