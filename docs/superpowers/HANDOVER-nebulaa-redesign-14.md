The owner watched `fx_ta.mp4` (the old 30 s Tamil video, made BEFORE the repetition fix, with captions added) and said: captions break, are not continuous, do not sync, and the Tamil text is wrong. Findings from a real transcription I ran (OpenAI `whisper-1`, `verbose_json`, segment and word timestamps; about one cent):
- OLD `ta.mp4` (30 s): the model really said different words from our line and repeated one ("வீட்டிருக்கே இலவச மதிப்பு" twice; it said "மதிப்பு" instead of "டெலிவரி") and the speech windows were 2.1 to 8.5 s, 9.7 to 18.5 s, 23.4 to 25.9 s. Our captions showed OUR intended text with ESTIMATED timing (start at 1 s of each clip), so they did not match what was said or when. That is the root cause: captions are not derived from the real audio.
- NEW `one.mp4` (10 s, after the fix): says "இன்றே ஆர்டர் செய்யுங்கள். ஆயிரம் ரூபாய்க்கு மேல் வீட்டிலேயே இலவச டெலிவரி." once, speech 0.0 to 5.5 s (so speech can start at 0 s, not 1 s). Whisper's own Tamil spelling is imperfect ("ஓடர்", "தீபாவலி"), so DISPLAY our intended text, not Whisper's.

## Update (2026-10-18, later): real-timing subtitles are built, proven by fakes only
**Built (branch nebulaa-redesign, local, not pushed):** subtitles are now timed from the real audio. `services/studioSubtitleTiming.js` (pure): the speech window of each clip (first segment start to last segment end inside that clip, clip offsets from the join, word times only tighten), our intended chunks spread over that window in proportion to length, held until the next, hidden when speech ends, neighbours merged when the window is too short, a similarity guard (consonant-skeleton Dice, `MIN_SIMILARITY` 0.3: fixtures say correct lines score 0.79 to 0.93, unrelated lines 0 to 0.25). `services/studioTranscribe.js`: the one real call (`whisper-1`, `verbose_json`, segment and word timestamps, key read at call time and only in the Authorization header, 60 s timeout, ONE attempt, refuses the real network under `node --test`). `services/studioVideoFinish.js` `timeSubtitles`: probe, spend check, audio extract (mono 16 kHz m4a), spend row WRITTEN FIRST (no row, no call), the call, cues. Any failure, timeout, no speech, switch off, ceiling reached or unsaved spend row gives NO subtitles and the notice "We could not time the subtitles, so this video has none."; spoken words that do not match give "The spoken words did not match the line, so this video has no subtitles." The video is never failed or refunded. The estimated captions (`buildCues`, start 1 s in) are gone. Subtitles stay OFF by default. Logs hold counts only.
