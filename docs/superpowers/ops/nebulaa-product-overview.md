# Nebulaa: product overview for creating ads and marketing copy

Last checked against the product: 22 October 2026. Written for someone with no technical background. Paste the whole file into ChatGPT (or any writing tool) before asking for ad concepts, scripts, landing copy or emails. Section 14 is a ready-made instruction you can paste after it.

**Status labels used throughout**
- **LIVE**: customers can use it today.
- **BUILT, OFF**: built and tested but not switched on in production yet. It is off on purpose (it uses paid outside services and still needs one small real test run), not broken. The owner can switch it on at any time. Do NOT advertise it until it is switched on and tried.
- **PARTLY**: works, with a known limit.
- **NOT READY**: not available. Never mention.

---

## 1. What Nebulaa is, in one paragraph
Nebulaa is a marketing platform that plans, creates and publishes a business's social media content, so the owner only has to review and say yes. It reads the business (website, answers to a few questions), builds a marketing plan, then produces the week's posts, carousels and short videos in the brand's own look and voice, in English and five Indian languages. Nothing goes live without the owner's approval. Pricing is built for Indian small businesses, from Rs 999 a month plus GST. There is also a done-for-you managed service run by Nebulaa's team.

**Headline on the website:** "Marketing on autopilot. You just say yes."
**Supporting line:** "Your marketing, run from one place, with you in charge."
**Brand name** is Nebulaa (the company is Noburo Systems Private Limited, India). The app address is gravity.nebulaa.ai.

## 2. Who it is for
- **Founders and owners** of small and growing businesses who do their own marketing and are short of time.
- **Marketing heads and CMOs** who need more content, faster, without losing brand control.
- **Agencies** that run content for several client brands and want to take on more clients without hiring.
- **Typical businesses:** D2C and retail brands, salons and clinics, restaurants and hospitality, coaching and schools, jewellery and fashion, B2B and professional services, creators.
- **Region:** built for India (rupee pricing, GST invoices, Indian festivals and languages).

## 3. How it works, in the order a customer sees it
1. **Sign up** and answer a few questions: what you sell, to whom (people, businesses or both), where your customers are, your goals, your brand voice, your languages.
2. **Brand Growth Blueprint (free).** Nebulaa reads the website and social pages and produces a marketing plan: who the ideal customer is, how to position the brand, where to show up. It is free and needs no card.
3. **My plan.** A visual strategy page: the business, products, customers, positioning, marketing mix, channels, content themes, a 90-day roadmap and the numbers to watch.
4. **Every week, posts and videos arrive** ready to review ("This week"), based on the plan.
5. **Review and approve.** The owner reads each item, changes it, and approves. Only approved items can be posted or scheduled.
6. **Publish and schedule** to the connected social accounts.
7. **Performance** shows what worked, and **what Nebulaa learned** feeds the next plan.

## 4. Features, one by one

### 4.1 Create content (LIVE)
- **Single post:** a picture with caption, hashtags and the right size for the platform.
- **Campaign:** a set of related posts around one idea or offer.
- **Carousel:** a swipeable multi-slide post (5 slides).
- **LinkedIn post:** written for a professional audience.
- **Idea box:** type an idea, speak it, or upload a pamphlet, poster or price list and Nebulaa reads it.
- **Options:** language, platforms, shape, logo position, how clean or rich the design looks.
- Everything uses the brand's own colours, logo, fonts and tone.

### 4.2 Videos (LIVE)
- Short videos made from a one-line idea, with real speech and music.
- **22 styles** (for example cinematic, product showcase, daily-life vlog, testimonial, unboxing, festive campaign, behind the scenes, educational).
- **Languages:** English, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam, or no speech.
- **Voice:** female or male. **Shape:** vertical (Reels, Shorts, Stories) or wide (YouTube, websites).
- **Length:** 10 seconds up to 60 seconds, made as connected 10-second parts that continue the same scene.
- **Optional finishing:** subtitles timed to the real speech (PARTLY: new; check each video), your logo, a closing line, a smooth join between parts.
- **Use your own people, place or product photos** as references so the video shows your real product.
- **Make again** and **change** options (changes work on 10-second videos).
- **Honest limits:** each 10 seconds takes 1 to 4 minutes to make; speech in Hindi, Telugu, Kannada and Malayalam is newer than English and Tamil, so check the line before posting.

### 4.3 Calendar (LIVE)
A monthly content plan with a theme, a cover and a date for each post. Festivals with fixed dates are included.

### 4.4 Review and approve, Library (LIVE)
- One place for everything made: needs approval, approved, scheduled, posted, archived, bin.
- The same actions everywhere: Approve, Schedule, Post now, Download, Edit, Make again, Archive, Delete (deleted items stay in the bin for 14 days).
- Posts Nebulaa made on its own need the owner's approval before they can go out.

### 4.5 Publish and schedule (LIVE, add-on)
Connect Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn and YouTube, then post now or schedule. **X (Twitter): NOT READY** (being connected). Google Business posting is limited.

### 4.6 My plan and the weekly plan (LIVE)
The strategy page described above, plus a weekly proposal of what to post, with a guided tour for new users.

### 4.7 Brand assets and brand memory (LIVE)
- **Brand assets:** logo, colours (read from the logo automatically), fonts, people, places, products.
- **What Nebulaa learned (brand memory):** it learns from the plan, the Blueprint, the customers, the products and how past posts performed, and uses this in the next posts. It shows what it learned and how much evidence is behind it. Owners can edit or delete anything.

### 4.8 Performance (LIVE)
Reach, engagement and followers per platform, the best posts, which formats and times work, and short findings with the number of posts behind each. A "make a post like this" button turns a finding into a new post. Numbers appear once accounts are connected and posts are measured.

### 4.9 Competitor insights (BUILT, OFF, add-on)
Track a few competitors weekly: their posts, best-performing content, and reply and comment draft helpers. Not switched on yet: it reads competitors through a paid scraping service and needs one real test pass first (switch: `ENABLE_COMPETITOR_TRACKER`). Do not advertise until it is on and tried.

### 4.10 Inbox (BUILT, OFF for marketing, add-on)
Comments and messages from Instagram and Facebook in one list, with a reply drafted for the owner to approve. Automatic sending is available only if the owner switches it on. **It has never been tested with real accounts, so do NOT advertise it** and never promise automatic replies.

### 4.11 Photoshoot (BUILT, OFF)
Ready-made packs of product photos. Not switched on yet: the per-photo price is an estimate and needs one real test run first (switch: `ENABLE_PHOTOSHOOT`). Do not advertise until it is on and tried.

### 4.12 Agency plan (LIVE)
- Run several **client brands** from one account: each client gets its own workspace (its own brand, plan, calendar, approvals).
- One shared pool of posts and videos across clients; a **usage report per client**.
- 5 client brands included; extra clients Rs 1,499 a month each (up to 25 in total).
- Publishing, inbox and competitor insights are part of the Agency plan (the last two are not live yet).

### 4.13 Managed service (LIVE)
Nebulaa's team can run the account for a client: a customer success manager works inside the client's account, topped up by the team. This is a service, not a self-serve plan.

### 4.14 Billing and account (LIVE)
Pay by UPI, cards or net banking. GST invoices (add your GSTIN). Cancel any time; a cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid month. Export and delete your account.

- **It knows your brand** (colours, logo, voice) and learns from what worked.
- **Made for India:** rupee pricing, GST invoices, Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and English.

## 8. What NOT to claim
- No results, percentages, "10x", revenue or follower promises. No customer numbers or testimonials unless you have real ones with permission. The line "built on real experience with 2,000+ MSMEs and startups" needs the owner's confirmation before use.
- Do not say it replaces a team, or that it posts without you.
- Do not promise automatic replies, competitor tracking, product photoshoots or X posting.
- Do not name the technology partners behind the AI or social posting.
- Do not say "free trial", "credits", or "7 days".
- Do not claim Meta, Google, Instagram or other brands endorse Nebulaa.

## 9. Tone of voice
Warm, plain, confident; short sentences; written for a busy owner, not a marketer. Talk about time saved and being in control. Avoid jargon (no "AI-powered synergies"). Indian-English spelling and rupee amounts.

## 10. Proof you can show in ads
- Screen recordings of: the Blueprint from a website, My plan, the weekly posts arriving, a video being made with subtitles, Review and approve, Schedule, Performance, and the Agency client switcher.
- A demo account with a full fake brand ("Zaya Naturals") exists for recording; it contains no real customer data.
- Videos and posts made with Nebulaa itself are the strongest proof ("this ad was made with Nebulaa").

- **How much does it cost?** From Rs 999 a month plus GST; the Blueprint is free.
