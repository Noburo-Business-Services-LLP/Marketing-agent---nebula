# The established demo agency, Brightwave Media (development only)

An Agency account that looks five months old, for recording the Agency demo. Everything is fictional and lives only in the development database (`nebulaa_dev`). The older two-client demo (`seed-demo-agency.js`, `demo-agency@example.test`) is a different account and is left alone.

## What it is
- **Agency:** Brightwave Media, Chennai. Agency plan, 5 monthly charges, one paid extra client (6 clients), billing history, its own marketing plan made on 20 October.
- **Six clients:** Brew & Beans Cafe (Coimbatore), Lotus Dental Care (Chennai), Urban Thread Boutique (Bengaluru), IronForge Fitness (Hyderabad), Skyline Homes Kochi, Pure Pantry Organics (Mumbai). Each joined at a different time.
- **Per client:** 19 to 62 published posts over the months, 3 scheduled, 2 waiting for approval, 4 to 7 short videos, results on every published post, daily follower numbers, two connected accounts (Instagram and Facebook, fake), a logo, and a plan that fills the This week card.
- **Shared pool:** the Quark ledger names the client on every spend, so Client usage shows posts, videos and Quarks per client for each month since joining and downloads as CSV. About 4,500 Quarks are left of this month's 6,500.
- **Pictures** are drawn on this computer (a drawing of each trade on brand colours, 4:5), and each video is a 10 second fade of three drawn scenes. Nothing is generated by a model. Files sit in `backend/demo-assets/agency/` (git-ignored; served by development servers only).

## Make, check, remove (from `backend`)

    node scripts/seed-established-agency.js create --password-file "/Users/<you>/nebulaa-demo-secrets/demo-agency-pro-login.txt"
    node scripts/seed-established-agency.js status
    node scripts/seed-established-agency.js remove

`create` again keeps the account and password and rebuilds the clients' rows so dates count back from today. The password is only ever in that file (first line email, second line password); read it with `cat`.

## Sign in
Use the DEVELOPMENT app (`http://localhost:3200` with the dev server on port 5000), never production and never the old app on port 3000. Do not make anything new while recording on the real dev server: generation there spends real provider money. To look around with every outside call blocked, use the safe pair in `demo-onboarding-recording.md` (backend on 5100, frontend on 3300 opened as 127.0.0.1).

## What to show
1. Clients: six cards with posts and videos this month. Open one (orange bar: Back to my clients).
2. Inside a client: Home (reach, followers, plan for the week, posts to approve), Library (Posted, Scheduled, Needs approval), Create > Video (Your videos), Insights.
3. Back to the agency Home: Client usage per client, Enough for about N posts or videos, month picker, CSV.
4. Plan and Settings > Billing: Agency active, the monthly invoices.

## What it does not show
- No real payment, no real social posts, no real video generation: the plan is a local record, the accounts are fake, the clips are drawn slideshows.
- Competitors, What Nebulaa learned, Inbox and Photoshoot are empty for the clients.
- The agency's own Home says to connect an account (the agency itself has none).
