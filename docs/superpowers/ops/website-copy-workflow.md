## How approval works
**Nothing goes out until you approve it.**
- You see every post first. Captions, pictures and videos wait in Review and approve. Change the words, make it again or delete it.
- You choose when it goes out. Approve a post and it is scheduled for its planned day, or post it now. Publishing needs the Publish and schedule add-on.
- Replies wait for you too. With the Inbox add-on, replies to comments and messages are drafted for you to read, change and approve. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]

## How it works: plan, create, approve, publish, learn
1. **Tell us about your business.** Answer a few simple questions and add your website and social pages. Nebulaa reads them and fills in most of it. You also get a free Brand Growth Blueprint.
2. **Get your plan.** See your ideal customer and a simple strategy to reach them, in My plan.
3. **Posts and videos every week.** Captions, pictures and short videos for the week are made for you and laid out on your calendar.

5. **Publish and schedule (add-on).** Connect your accounts and send approved posts out now, or on the day you pick.
6. **Reply from one inbox (add-on).** Comments and messages from your connected pages arrive in one list, each with a reply drafted for you to approve. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]
7. **Learn what works.** See reach, engagement and new followers for your posts. Nebulaa keeps notes on what works for your business. Competitor insights is an add-on.

Start with step one. It takes a few questions. You can change every answer later.

## Questions people ask
1. **How does pricing work?** Each plan adds Quarks every month: 600 on Starter, 2,000 on Growth and 6,000 on Agency. Quarks are what you spend to make posts, carousels, videos and photos, and every price is shown before you make something. Plan Quarks are for the month you paid for; top-ups stay valid for 60 days. Prices are shown without GST; 18% GST is added at checkout.
2. **What needs my approval?** Every post. Captions, pictures and videos wait in Review and approve, and nothing is published or scheduled before you approve it. With the Inbox add-on, replies are drafted for you to approve too. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]
3. **Where can my posts go?** With the Publish and schedule add-on you connect your accounts and send approved posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Google Business, and videos to YouTube. You can also download any post and share it yourself.
4. **Which languages does it write in?** English and Tamil today, on their own or mixed in one post. We add more languages only after our team can check them.
5. **Do I need a designer or a website?** No. Nebulaa writes the captions and makes the pictures and videos. If you have a website, add it and Nebulaa fills in most of your details. If not, answer a few simple questions.
6. **What happens to my data?** Your details, photos and posts are used to make your content, and we do not sell your personal data. You can download your data and delete your account yourself in Settings, under Security. (Link: Privacy Policy.)
7. **Can I cancel any time?** Yes. Go to Settings, then Billing, and choose Cancel plan. Your plan stays available until the end of the month you paid for, and you are not charged again. (Link: Refund Policy.)
8. **Does it work for agencies?** Yes, on the Agency plan: 5 client brands included, one shared pool of posts and videos for all clients, and a usage report for each client. Extra client brands are Rs 1,499 a month plus GST.
