## How it works, in seven steps

**1. Tell us about your business.** Answer a few simple questions and add your website and social pages. Nebulaa reads them and fills in most of the rest. You also get a free Brand Growth Blueprint.

**2. Get your plan.** See who your ideal customer is and a simple strategy to reach them, in My plan.

**3. Every week, posts and videos made for you.** Captions, images and short videos for the week appear in This week and Create, and are laid out on your calendar.

**4. Review and approve.** Check each post on your phone, change what you like, and approve it. Nothing goes out without you.

**5. Publish and schedule (add-on).** Connect your Instagram and Facebook pages and send approved posts out now, or at a time you pick.

**6. Reply from one inbox (add-on).** Comments and messages from your connected pages arrive in one list. Each one has a reply drafted for you to read, change and approve. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST] Nothing is sent for you unless you choose to switch automatic sending on. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]

**7. See what competitors do and what your audience says (add-ons).** Look at what businesses like yours post and what works for them. See what your own audience says in the comments on your posts. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST] (The comments part comes with the Inbox add-on.)

## Questions people ask

**1. Do I need design or writing skills?**
No. Nebulaa writes the captions and makes the images and videos. You only check them and press approve.

**2. Which languages does it write in?**
English, Tamil, Hindi, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam and more. You choose when you sign up and can change it any time.

**3. Do I need a website?**
No. If you have one, add it and Nebulaa fills in most of your details. If you do not, answer a few simple questions.

**4. Where do my posts go?**
You connect your Instagram and Facebook pages with the Publish and schedule add-on. A post goes out only after you approve it.

**5. Will replies go out without my approval?**
No. With the Inbox add-on, Nebulaa drafts a reply and you read it, change it and approve it. Automatic sending is off until you switch it on yourself. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]

**6. What if I run out of posts or videos?**
Running low? Add more posts and videos any time from inside the app. Top-ups stay valid for 60 days. Prices are shown without GST; 18% GST is added at checkout.

**7. Can I run several clients from one account?**
Yes, on the Agency plan. You get 5 client brands, one shared pool of posts and videos, and a usage report for each client. Extra client brands are Rs 1,499 a month plus GST.

**8. Can I see what my competitors are posting?**
Yes, with the Competitor insights add-on you can look at what businesses like yours post and what works for them.
