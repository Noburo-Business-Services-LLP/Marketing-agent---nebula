## How it works: How Nebulaa runs your marketing
**How Nebulaa runs your marketing.** Eight stages that work as one loop. What Nebulaa learns from your results goes into your next plan and your next content.

1. **Understand your business.** Add your website and social pages, and answer a few questions. Your free Brand Growth Blueprint shows what Nebulaa confirmed.
2. **Know your market and customers.** See your ideal customers, how to position your brand and where to show up. It all sits in My plan.
3. **Watch your competitors (Add-on).** Follow the competitor pages you confirm. See what they post and which of their posts worked.
4. **Plan.** Get a roadmap for the next 90 days and a monthly content calendar. You see what is planned for each day.
5. **Create.** Posts, carousels, campaigns, LinkedIn posts and short videos in your brand style. Product photoshoots are coming soon.
6. **Review, schedule and publish (Publish add-on).** Nothing goes out until you approve it. With the Publish add-on, approved content goes out on the day you pick.
7. **Engage (Inbox add-on).** Comments and messages arrive in one inbox. Each reply is drafted for you to read, change and approve. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST] (If the Inbox test has not passed, show "Coming soon" with: Comments and messages in one inbox, with replies drafted for you to approve.)
8. **Learn and improve.** See results for each platform and your top posts. Nebulaa learns from your plan, products, results and competitors, and uses it next time.

Then the loop starts again, with what Nebulaa learned.

## Questions people ask
1. **Is this only for social media posts?** No. Nebulaa covers your marketing from start to finish: it gets to know your business and customers, makes your plan and monthly calendar, creates posts, carousels, campaigns and short videos, and learns from your results. Publishing, competitor insights and the inbox are add-ons on Starter and Growth, and are included in Agency. With the Inbox add-on, replies to comments and messages are drafted for you to approve. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]
2. **Do I stay in control?** Yes. Nebulaa does the work and you make the decisions. Nothing is published or scheduled until you approve it, you can change or delete anything before then, and you can update your business details at any time.
3. **What needs my approval?** Everything that goes out. Captions, pictures and videos wait in Review and approve, and nothing is published or scheduled before you approve it. With the Inbox add-on, replies are drafted for you to approve too. [CONFIRM AFTER INBOX TEST]
4. **How does pricing work?** Each plan adds Quarks every month: 600 on Starter, 2,000 on Growth and 6,000 on Agency. Quarks are what you spend to make posts, carousels, videos and photos, and every price is shown before you make something. Plan Quarks are for the month you paid for; top-ups stay valid for 60 days. Prices are shown without GST; 18% GST is added at checkout.
5. **What does Nebulaa learn about my business?** It learns from six sources: your plan, your Blueprint, your customers, your products, your results and, with the add-on, your competitors. You can see what it holds under What Nebulaa learned, and it uses this for your next plan and content.
6. **Can it track my competitors?** Yes, with the Competitor insights add-on (included in Agency). You confirm which competitor pages to follow, and Nebulaa reads only their public posts to show what they post, which posts did well and the trends coming up.
7. **Where can my posts go?** With the Publish and schedule add-on you connect your accounts and send approved posts to Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, X and Google Business, and videos to YouTube. You can also download any post and share it yourself.
8. **Which languages does it write in?** English and Tamil today, on their own or mixed in one post. We add more languages only after our team can check them.
9. **Do I need a designer or a website?** No. Nebulaa writes the words and makes the pictures and videos. If you have a website, add it and Nebulaa fills in most of your details. If not, answer a few simple questions.
10. **What happens to my data?** Your details, photos and posts are used to make your content, and we do not sell your personal data. You can download your data and delete your account yourself in Settings, under Security. (Link: Privacy Policy.)
11. **Can I cancel any time?** Yes. Go to Settings, then Billing, and choose Cancel plan. Your plan stays available until the end of the month you paid for, and you are not charged again. (Link: Refund Policy.)
12. **Does it work for agencies?** Yes, on the Agency plan: 5 client brands included, one shared pool of posts and videos for all clients, and a usage report for each client. Extra client brands are Rs 1,499 a month plus GST.
