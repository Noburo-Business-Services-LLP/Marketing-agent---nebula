# Final whole-branch review, part A: the money path (Agency plan)

Reviewer: Opus 5.5, read-only, 2026-10-16. Scope: quarkBuckets, trialGuard, creditGuard, agency.js, billingPlans, payment.js, apiCosts/vendorPlans, studioVideos, credits.js, every `credits.balance` reader and writer, creditAccessGuard test. `node --test tests/agency*.test.js tests/creditAccessGuard.test.js`: 175 pass, 0 fail.

## Verdict

The core money path is sound. Every client spend goes through `quarkBuckets.spend`, which charges the agency in one conditional update that also checks the agency can pay (`CLIENT_PAYER_FILTER`). Nothing is ever charged to a client record. Refunds go back to the recorded payer, or to the agency the client belongs to now. Razorpay handling fails closed, is idempotent per payment id, and checks the amount strictly against the price stored on the record, on both verify and webhook. The slot cap, the one-time limit decrease, the never-below-zero clamp, freezing (never deleting), and cancelling slots when the plan ends all hold. Money that is taken but not applied is recorded (`needsRefund`) and emailed once.

There are no Critical findings. Two Important defects should be fixed before launch: the staff Usage page crashes, and refused payments can be invoiced. One Important race is older than this work but now also covers the Agency plan. One Important design point needs the owner's call.

## Important

**I1. The staff Usage page returns 500 once any account is on the Agency plan.** `backend/services/staff/usage.js:176-180`. `topByTierOf` builds tiers from `['free','starter','professional','managed']`, then runs `byId[resolveTier(u)].clients += 1`. For an agency owner (tier `agency`, not hidden, so it counts as a customer), `byId.agency` is undefined and this throws. Reproduced: `buildUsage({users:[{_id:'a',plan:{tier:'agency'}}]})` throws "Cannot read properties of undefined (reading 'clients')". The route catches it and returns 500, so the whole Usage page is gone for every staff member as soon as the first agency signs up. Fix: add `agency` to `ids` and `labels`, or skip unknown tiers. Confidence: high, reproduced.

**I2. `POST /api/payment/retry-invoices` makes Zoho tax invoices for refused payments.** `backend/routes/payment.js:923-948`. The loop invoices every payment row that has no `invoiceUrl`. Rows written by `recordRefusal` (status `failed`, `needsRefund`, no `exGstAmount`, no invoice) qualify, so they fall into the `{ amount: payment.amount }` branch. Scenario: an agency pays for a 21st slot and is refused (`at_limit`), or pays a mismatched amount. The agency (or the frontend) then calls retry-invoices, and a GST invoice is issued for money we are about to refund. Any signed-in owner can call this route; it is blocked only from inside a client. The same applies to `refunded` rows (that part is older). Fix: skip rows whose `status !== 'paid'`. Confidence: high.

**I3. A verify/webhook race can wipe a month's allowance. Older than this work, but now also on the Agency plan's first payment and renewals.** `backend/routes/payment.js:243-246`. `applyCharge` runs `quarkBuckets.settle(user._id, now, {forcePlan:true})` before its idempotent update, and decides "not already applied" from a `user` it read earlier. Scenario: the checkout's verify-subscription and the `subscription.charged` webhook arrive together (normal for a first payment). The webhook reads the user (no payment row yet). Verify then grants: `planBalance = 6000`, `balance += 6000`. The webhook's `settle(forcePlan)` reads the new document and expires `planBalance` (6000). The webhook's own update then matches nothing (`$ne paymentId`), so it returns `already_processed`. The customer paid Rs 6,999 + GST and their 6,000 Quarks are gone at once, with only a `plan_quarks_expired` ledger row to show for it. The window is a few round trips. Two concurrent deliveries of the same renewal webhook hit it the same way. Fix: make the expiry part of the same guarded update (a pipeline update that sets `planBalance` to the grant and adjusts `balance` by `grant - currentPlan`, all under the `$ne paymentId` filter), or re-check the payment id right before settling. Confidence: medium-high that the race is real; low-medium that it happens often.

**I4. Slot Quarks land in the top-up bucket, so top-ups effectively never expire while any slot renews (owner decision).** `backend/services/billingPlans.js:204-209` with `quarkBuckets.topupGrantUpdate` (`:188-193`). Each slot cycle grants 500 Quarks as a top-up and resets `credits.topupExpiresAt` to now + 60 days for the whole top-up bucket. With even one slot renewing monthly, slot Quarks and every bought pack roll over forever. At 20 slots that is 10,000 Quarks a month that build up as unexpiring liability. The spec says plan Quarks end with the month and top-ups after 60 days, and lists the 500 next to the monthly price, which reads as a monthly allowance. The brief chose the top-up bucket on purpose (the plan bucket would be wiped by the plan's renewal), so this is a design gap, not a coding slip. Options: give slot Quarks their own monthly expiry, or add them to `planBalance` on each slot cycle without moving `topupExpiresAt`. Confidence: high on the behaviour, medium on whether the owner wants it.

## Minor

- **M1. The frozen-client race (question 2) cannot be exploited.** `quarkBuckets.js:89-97`. `frozen` comes from a separate read before the atomic payer update, so only requests already in flight when `syncWorkspacePlans` freezes a client can still spend: at most one request each. They spend the agency's own pool, so Nebulaa loses nothing. The case where Nebulaa would lose money (plan ended) is enforced atomically by `CLIENT_PAYER_FILTER` (`plan.tier: 'agency'`). Moving `agency.frozen` into the update would need a second document, so leave it.
- **M2. Removal race can lose one job's refund.** `agencyWorkspaces.js:124-133` together with `routes/agency.js:98`. `removeWorkspace` pauses the client, checks running work, then purges. A request that passed `resolvePayer` before the pause can still charge and create a job after the check. Separately, `GET /api/agency/clients` runs `syncWorkspacePlans`, which can unfreeze the client in the middle of a removal. In both cases the job's later refund goes through `resolvePayer`, finds no client, and is dropped. The agency loses at most one job's Quarks. Possible fix: check `runningWork` twice with a short gap, or make the sync skip a client that is being removed.
- **M3. A late `subscription.charged` can bring back a cancelled slot.** `payment.js:237-241`. `firstCycle = record.active !== true`. If Razorpay delivers a charge for a slot after its `subscription.cancelled` (webhook order is not guaranteed; retries run for up to 24 h), the charge is treated as a new first payment. `extraSlots` goes up by 1, the record becomes active, and no ending event will ever arrive to lower it again. The agency then keeps a free client slot. It needs a delayed charge, so it is unlikely. Possible fix: mark slot records `ended: true` on an ending event and refuse first-cycle grants on ended records.
- **M4. Agency plan amount mismatch is not cancelled.** `payment.js:604-609` and `534-537`. A mismatched Agency **plan** charge is recorded and emailed but never cancelled at Razorpay (slots are). A misconfigured Razorpay plan would bill the customer the wrong amount every month, with one refund email each time. Consider cancelling, as `refuseSlot` does.
- **M5. A slot can be bought while the plan is set to end.** `payment.js:462-470` and `canBuySlot`. Nothing refuses a slot purchase when `subscription.cancelAtPeriodEnd` is set. The slot is charged a full month and then cancelled immediately when the plan ends. Also, `cancel-subscription` cancels slots at the end of each slot's own cycle, which can fall after the plan's end, so that part of the month is paid and not used.
- **M6. False "cancel it in Razorpay" alerts after a normal cancellation.** `payment.js:121-127`. After a user cancellation, `cancelAddonSubscriptions` calls `cancel(id, 0)` again on slots that are already cancelled, and raises the alert when Razorpay errors. Apply the `alreadyDone` check used at `:816`.
- **M7. Staff can add Quarks to a client record, where they can never be used.** `routes/staff.js:331-339` and `toolsRepo.grantQuarks` (`:112`). `clientFor` checks `isCustomer`, which does not exclude hidden workspaces (already flagged: staff can open a workspace by id). A staff grant to a client lands on the client record, where `walletBalance` never reads it, so the Quarks are lost and are still counted as "granted" in Money. Refuse `agency.ownerId` in `clientFor`. Bulk actions already exclude workspaces (`staff.js:479`).
- **M8. Gaps in the staff revenue numbers.** `services/staff/recurring.js:48-51` and `money.js renewalsOf`. Online MRR and the upcoming-renewals list count only the plan, at today's price. Slot subscriptions (Rs 1,499 each) and stored `expectedPaise` are ignored, so Agency revenue is under-reported.
- **M9. `HERO_LIMITS` has no `agency` key.** `config/entitlements.js:21`. `heroLimitForUser` returns `undefined` for agency owners and clients. Nothing is blocked today (`used >= undefined` is false), but this works by accident, and the field is left out of the API reply instead of being `null`. Add `agency: Infinity`.
- **M10. Display only: frozen clients see the pool balance.** `routes/credits.js:12-16`. `holderOf` ignores `frozen`, so `/api/credits` would show a frozen client the agency's pool. The pass check refuses frozen clients, so this is display only and unlikely to be reached.

## Checked and found correct

- **Spending.** All spend paths go through `quarkBuckets.spend`: trialGuard and creditGuard `deductCredits`, studioVideos, hero, Blueprint. The `credits.balance` writers outside it are payment grants, staff grants (M7), and the social reward (`social.js:55` returns early for workspaces). `checkTrial`, `requireCredits` and `checkCredits` read the payer's balance through `walletBalance`: 0 when the client is frozen or the agency cannot pay.
- **Refunds.** Studio video and reference refunds, hero, and Blueprint pass the recorded `payerId`. The others resolve the payer at refund time, which still points at the agency after the plan ends or the client is frozen. Removal is refused while 7 kinds of charged work run (M2 is the gap). A studio video refund is claimed once (`refunded:false` filter).
- **Missing payer.** If the payer is missing or the client is gone, spend is refused (`payerId` null) and nothing is charged elsewhere.
- **Razorpay checks.** The webhook returns 503 without a secret and 400 on a bad signature; it needs a raw Buffer and uses a timing-safe compare. Idempotency is per payment id, in the same atomic filter. A refused payment stays refused on replay and is not re-recorded or re-emailed. Verify and webhook both compare against `priceOfRecord`/`expectedPaiseOf` (stored at purchase), and Quarks come from config only. `recordMatches` checks the Razorpay plan id and the owner in notes.
- **Slots.** The cap is enforced atomically (`extraSlots < 20` in the gate), and two racing last-slot payments resolve to one grant and one `at_limit` refusal. A renewal adds Quarks only. Ending a slot is filtered on `active:true` and clamped at 0. Plan end cancels slots, runs `endSlot` on each, and syncs (all clients frozen at limit 0). A later slot renewal is refused (`no_agency_plan`), recorded and cancelled.
- **Managed and existing accounts.** They are not blocked or changed. Their spend and refund filters are unchanged apart from one extra read. `create-subscription` refuses managed accounts as before. Agency rules apply only when `agency.ownerId` is set or the tier is `agency`.
- **Entitlements.** `addonsOf` gives `bundle` to tier `agency`, and clients mirror it. Add-ons are refused for agency. `TIERS`, money `TIER_LABELS`, `INVOICE_ITEMS.agency` and `.slot`, and `describePayment` all handle agency. Staff bulk `set_plan` cannot set `agency` (by design) and skips workspaces.
