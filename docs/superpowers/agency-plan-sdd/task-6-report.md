## How the webhook finds and verifies
1. No `RAZORPAY_WEBHOOK_SECRET` -> 503, nothing granted. Bad or missing signature -> 400.
2. Owner = `User.findOne({'plan.subscriptions.subscriptionId': id})`. The STORED record decides what the subscription is for (kind `plan`, `addon`, `slot`; key must be a known plan, add-on or `client`). `recordMatches` also needs the event's `plan_id` to equal the Razorpay plan id stored when we created it, and `notes.userId` to equal the owner. Anything else is ignored with 200 and grants nothing.
3. `subscription.charged`: for the agency plan and slots the paid amount must equal exactly the configured price plus GST in paise (missing or different = nothing granted, owner alert). This is stricter than Starter/Growth on purpose (a wrongly configured `RAZORPAY_PLAN_ID_SLOT` or `_AGENCY` env value would otherwise grant the wrong thing); Starter, Growth and add-ons behave exactly as before.
4. Quarks and slots come from config only. Idempotency: the one atomic update has `payments.razorpayPaymentId: {$ne: paymentId}`, so a replay matches nothing (also for verify plus webhook for the same payment).
5. Slot extras in the SAME atomic filter: `plan.tier: 'agency'`, `agency.ownerId: null` (never a workspace), the stored record active or inactive as expected (`$elemMatch`), and for a first payment `agency.extraSlots` missing or `< MAX_EXTRA_SLOTS` (20). First payment = record not yet active: adds `agency.extraSlots +1` and sets the record active in one update, with the slot's Quarks into the top-up bucket (60 day expiry). Renewal = record already active: Quarks only, never another slot. Two different payments racing on an inactive record: the loser's filter fails, it re-reads once and applies as a renewal (one slot, two Quarks grants).
6. A slot payment that cannot be honoured (agency plan not active, the account is a workspace, already at the most clients, unresolved race) grants nothing, writes no payment row, raises an owner alert naming the payment id ("refund it in Razorpay"), and cancels that slot subscription at Razorpay so it does not charge again. The customer sees the 409 on verify.

## Limits and freezing on cancel
- `agency.extraSlots` is the only stored number; the limit is always `clientLimitOf` = min(25, 5 + extraSlots). Nothing is deleted anywhere.
- Slot cancelled, halted or completed (webhook): atomic `$inc -1` plus record inactive, only if the record was active (a repeated event, a halted after a cancelled, or a never-paid slot changes nothing). A clamp resets a negative value to 0. Then `syncWorkspacePlans` freezes the newest clients above the new limit (oldest kept). `agency.clientCount` stays; the agency must remove clients itself (create is refused while `clientCount >= limit`). Buying a slot again unfreezes them.
- Agency plan cancelled, halted or completed: tier goes to free (existing code), each active slot subscription is cancelled at Razorpay (immediately), marked inactive and decremented exactly once (their later own events do nothing), the owner is alerted if a cancel call fails, then `syncWorkspacePlans` sets every client to the free tier and freezes all (limit 0). Pass checks and the shared pool already refuse a non-agency owner (Tasks 4 and 5). Buying the plan again re-syncs and unfreezes the clients that fit (extra slots are gone with the old subscription and must be bought again).
- Welcome email: once per account on the first applied agency payment; a slot sends none.
