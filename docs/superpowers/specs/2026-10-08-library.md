# Library: one place for everything you have made

Date: 2026-10-08. Status: owner approved the idea and the name ("Library"); this is the spec for the first version. A preview with sample data comes before any release.

## Problem
Finished work is spread over three places with overlapping tabs:
- Review and approve (`/drafts`): Needs review, All, Drafts, Scheduled, Posted, Archived, for posts and images.
- Videos (`/reels`): All AI Videos, Drafts, Created, Scheduled, Posted, for step-by-step videos.
- Hero Studio (`/reels/hero`): its own list of Hero videos.
Adding another list would make it worse.

## Decision
One page, **Library** (`/library`, left menu, after Review and approve), where images and videos are browsed together.

## What it shows
- A toggle at the top: **All, Images, Videos**.
- Filters next to it: **status** (All, Drafts, Scheduled, Posted, Archived), **platform**, and for videos **type** (Hero video, Step-by-step video). Search by title.
- One card for everything: thumbnail (still frame for a video, with a small play badge), short title, date, status tag, platform icons.
- Newest first. A "creating" card with the generation clock for anything still generating.

## What clicking does
- Finished video or image: a preview sheet with the media, caption, platforms, **Download**, and **Open** (takes you to the screen that owns it).
- Finished Hero video: the same sheet also has **Post now / Schedule** (the component already built for the Hero page).
- Draft that is not finished: **Edit** opens the wizard where it stopped (video draft: `/reels?jobId=`; post draft: Review and approve).
- Delete stays in the owning screen for now.

## Data (no new backend in version 1)
Three existing lists are fetched together in the page and adapted to one shape (`utils/library.ts`, pure and tested):
- Post and image drafts: `GET /api/drafts` (reels excluded there already).
- Step-by-step videos: `GET /api/video-generation/drafts`.
- Hero videos: `GET /api/hero-video/jobs` (title and thumbnail already returned).
Sorting and filtering happen in the page over the merged list (first 20 to 50 of each). A failed source shows "could not load videos" and the rest still show. Unified server-side paging is a later step.

## What stays and what moves (phase 2, after the owner has used it)
- Review and approve keeps **Needs review** as its main job; its All, Drafts, Scheduled, Posted and Archived tabs are replaced by a link to Library.
- Videos keeps Create (the wizard and the Hero entry); its list tabs are replaced by a link to Library.
Nothing is removed in version 1, so nobody loses a screen while Library is being judged.

## Tests
- `utils/library.ts`: each adapter (post draft, video draft, Hero job) gives the same card shape; status mapping; filters by kind, status, platform and search; newest first; items without media still get a safe card.
- Preview with sample data of all three kinds before release.

## Not in scope
Bulk actions, folders or tags, unified server paging, posting from the sheet for step-by-step videos (use Open).
